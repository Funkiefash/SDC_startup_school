What is SDC Startup School?

It is a pan-african startup school designed to help first-time and aspiring founders navigate the journey from idea to successful startup.

We are aimed at bridging the startup education gap that exists within emerging economies around the world, starting with Africa.

We are on a mission to educate and equip aspiring and first-time founders with the required startup education.

SDC Startup School is a program that helps entrepreneurs turn ideas into successful startups.

FREE

We believe that everyone deserves a shot at building their dream startup, and cost shouldn't be a barrier. That's why SDC Startup School is totally free. No hidden fees, no strings attached. Dive into our classes and start learning without worrying about your wallet.

SELF-PACED

Life's busy, we get it. With SDC Startup School, you're in control. Our self-paced program lets you learn whenever it suits you. Whether you're juggling work, family, or other commitments, you can go through the modules at your own speed. Take your time, and make the learning experience work for you.

ONLINE

Learn from anywhere! SDC Startup School is 100% online, so you can access all the classes from the comfort of your home or on the go. All you need is an internet connection to access all our lessons, case studies, and more. It's flexible, convenient, and designed to fit into your lifestyle.

RICH CURRICULUM

Our curriculum is rich, structured and comprehensive, touching on all the key areas of the startup world. No more piecing together random YouTube videos—everything you need is in one place. From understanding the basics of what a startup truly is to mastering funding, marketing, and scaling, we've got you covered.
